# Watercraft

From Kenney's **Watercraft Kit** (https://kenney.nl, CC0 1.0). `boat-fishing-small.glb` is the boat that
circles the island (`src/sea.js`) and is moored at the quay (`src/island.js`). Both use the cached
loader in `src/watercraft.js`, scaled 0.23 and textured with `Textures/colormap.png`; the model,
materials and atlas are shared. The remaining watercraft models are available for future use.
