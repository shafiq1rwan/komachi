Drag pan · Scroll zoom · Q / E turn camera · WASD move
Streets tool: drag to draw a street. Pick a zone, then click & drag across up to 3 cells beside a street to make one block.
Clear takes buildings away; click or drag along a street to remove it (one a building opens onto stays)
Civic: one cell for a substation, water works or recycling centre, two or three for a public bath. Nothing depends on them; the town shows their effect.
Parking: one or two cells beside a street; homes and workplaces nearby park there instead of lining the kerb
Hover to inspect · R turns a building · click a name to follow someone

Welcome to Komachi

An empty island is waiting. Zone a few homes, a shop or two and somewhere to work. Roads will find their own way around each block. Then sit back and watch your little town come alive.
