# itch devlog: Play Komachi free in your browser

Written 2026-10-07 for the itch page. Title then body; post it when the demo file is live and the Run button shows logged out.

**Title:** Try Komachi in your browser before you buy

**Body:**

You can now play Komachi right here on this page, free, no download. Press Run.

It is the whole game, not a cut-down one: the island, the guided first town, every zone tool and building, the crews arriving
by train, households moving in, traffic, weather and seasons, the festival, the ferry, farming, and the fishing game at the
quay with the catch held up for the camera.

The demo keeps one town for as long as the tab is open and gives you three days in it, about a quarter of an hour, which is
enough to draw your first streets, see the first family arrive and catch something off the jetty. On day four the town pauses
and asks whether you would like to keep it.

The full game, $4.99, is what carries on from there: as many towns as you like, kept between visits; export and import of
towns as files; photos saved to your device; the desktop app for Windows and Linux that runs offline; and every update since
launch, with the next ones included.

A few small things also went in this week: the info card now opens on a click instead of popping up under the mouse, with a
calm outline round whatever you point at; residents ride little cargo tricycles; and the line at the quay brings up a tin
can now and then, as well as the old boot.

If the demo runs slowly on your machine, give it twenty seconds at the start while the browser warms up, and try the Settings
page for a lower quality preset. Tell me what you think in the comments; I read all of them.
