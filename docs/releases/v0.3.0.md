## GitHub Release: Komachi 0.3.0 — A guided start, and a fish on the line

Komachi's first big update since launch. Everything below is in the Windows installer, the portable exe and the
Linux AppImage attached here, and in the web version on itch.

**A guided first town.** A new island now walks you through its first street, two homes, a shop and a workplace,
one gentle prompt at a time, each cleared by simply doing the thing. Then it shows you the first crew stepping
off the train and the first family moving in, and leaves you to it. The prompts never return on a town that
already has buildings.

**Fishing at the quay.** When a resident is out on the jetty with a rod, "Join the anglers" lets you fish as them.
Cast, watch the float, strike at the bite, then hold to reel and let go to give line while the fish circles under
the water. Land it and they hold it up for the camera; lose it and they slump. The old boot is still down there.
A new fishing card shows line tension, reeling progress and a word of advice, with small sound cues.

**A smoother opening.** The train ride that starts every town runs at the full frame rate now, with a round iris
wipe instead of a repainted gradient.

**Also in this version**
- Photo mode and a photo album per town, with captions and save-to-device.
- Towns menu with export and import of a town as a file, keyboard-complete menus, "Saved just now" on the pause card.
- A privacy notice page: the game collects nothing.
- Builders' queue, working hours, service vehicles, the kitsune at the shrine, the harbour island and much more from
  the 0.2.x series; the full list is in CHANGELOG.md.

**Price.** With this update the launch price ends and Komachi is $4.99. Everyone who bought it earlier keeps it, updates
included. The desktop app does not update itself: download the new installer from the same itch page, and your towns
stay where they are.

Known limits: Windows and Linux only (the macOS build was dropped until it can be signed). Fishing on a slow laptop can
feel sluggish while the browser compiles shaders in the first seconds at the quay.

## Reddit post (r/CozyGamers, r/IndieDev, r/WebGames; title then body)

**Title:** My cosy Japanese-town sim just got a guided start and a fishing game you play as one of the residents

**Body:**

A week ago I posted Komachi here, a small observational town builder set on a Japanese island. You draw streets, zone
blocks, and watch households arrive by train, open shops, bike to work and gossip on benches. Some of you said the first
ten minutes were confusing and a couple of you asked what the price would become. This update answers both.

What's new in 0.3.0:

- **A guided first town.** A new island now prompts you through its first street, two homes, a shop and a workplace,
  one card at a time. Each one clears when you do the thing, no "OK" buttons. Then you watch the first crew step off
  the train and the first family move in, and it leaves you alone for good.
- **Fishing at the quay, as a resident.** When someone from your town is out on the jetty, you can join them and fish
  as them. Strike at the bite, hold to reel, let go to give line while the shadow circles under the water. Land it and
  they hold it up for the camera. Lose it and they slump. There is also an old boot.
- **A smoother opening** train ride, a photo album per town, export and import of towns as files, fully keyboard
  menus.

It runs in the browser, and there is a Windows installer, a portable exe and a Linux AppImage. Your towns save locally
and nothing is collected.

On price: it launched at $2 as a pre-release price and is $4.99 from today. Everyone who bought it early keeps it with
all updates. Next on the list are interiors you can furnish and a second city theme.

Link in the comments, and I would love to hear what the guided start feels like to someone who has never seen the game.
