Komachi privacy notice

Last updated 2 October 2026 · Komachi is made by Saiss

The short version

Komachi does not collect, store or send any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking and no analytics in the game itself.

What stays on your device

  • Your towns, photos and settings are saved in your browser's storage (or the app's own folder on desktop). They never leave your device unless you choose to export a town as a file.
  • Nothing you build, name or photograph is sent to Komachi's maker or anyone else.

Where the game is played on a portal

When Komachi is played on a games portal such as Poki, the portal's own pages may show advertising and use analytics under that portal's privacy policy, which applies to the page around the game. Komachi itself adds no tracking of its own. On Poki, see poki.com/en/privacy.

Children

Komachi is suitable for all ages and asks for no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this notice ever changes, the date above will change with it. Questions can be left on the game's page on itch.io.
